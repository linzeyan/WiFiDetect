WiFiDetect for Mac includes the open source packages below, each under the Apache License 2.0. The NOTICE files of those that have one follow, as that license asks, and then the MIT License of llhttp, which SwiftNIO includes. WiFiDetect for iPhone and iPad includes none of them.

- [swift-nio-http2](https://github.com/apple/swift-nio-http2)
